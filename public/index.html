THE REWARD

Play, reflect,
find your next step.

The demo generates a personal report from the player’s results. It compares decision accuracy for Site, Technical, Planning and QAQC; BIM completion is recorded separately. Use it to start a conversation, not as a career aptitude test.

Planned event reward: a custom 3D-printed role badge with a QR code on the back linking to BCA course information for that role. Badge production and the role-specific QR handout are planned.

JEWEL:
THE LAST TIMELINE

Inspired by Avengers: Endgame, a team travels back to fix engineering problems and bring the waterfall back to life.

In this fictional story, a past construction mistake stops the Rain Vortex. The students must find and check the problems before returning to the future with their keys.

The story, faults and time travel are fictional; they are not claims about the real Jewel.

EVENT DAY SETUP · PLANNED

Nine students.
One team.

Nine students play at the same time on nine individual PCs: one student leader and eight engineering role players.

01 / LEADER

Guide the team.

The leader hosts, operates the time machine, coordinates progress and receives the keys.

02 / EIGHT ENGINEERS

Work on your mission.

Each student takes a different role and completes an independent mission in the past.

03 / PEER SUPPORT

Share what you know.

Students who finish early can share clues and help teammates. Direct control of another player’s mission has not been verified.

Current status: Eight scoped missions, the team ending and a Host/Join foundation exist in the prototype. The full nine-PC venue LAN setup still needs testing.

EIGHT ENGINEERING ROLES

Different tasks.
One shared goal.

Each role checks its work and earns a unique key. The images show actual prototype mission introductions; the dates are fictional.

THE ENDING

Eight keys.
One last action.

  1. Complete and verify each mission to collect a unique key.
  2. Use the watch to travel back to the future.
  3. Hand the keys to the leader.
  4. The leader uses all eight keys to open the secret door, then presses the hidden button to restore the waterfall.
Presenting today: The current local one-PC Presentation Demo lets a presenter approach a role and enter its mission. Returning or restarting a mission in this mode does not collect team keys. The older public Windows download does not contain every feature shown here.
